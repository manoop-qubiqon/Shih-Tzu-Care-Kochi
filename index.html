About the center

Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Talk to us — we're happy to help.

How often should a Shih Tzu be groomed?

Most Shih Tzus do best with a full groom every 4–6 weeks, plus regular brushing at home to prevent matting. Puppies and long-coat dogs may benefit from more frequent visits.

Do you accept puppies?

Yes. We offer extra-gentle puppy grooming to help young dogs get comfortable with the process early. We'll go at their pace.

Do you provide boarding?

We do — cosy, supervised overnight and multi-day stays with 24/7 monitoring, so your Shih Tzu is safe and comfortable while you're away.

Do you accept rescued dogs?

Absolutely. Our shelter initiative provides temporary shelter, rehabilitation and adoption support for Shih Tzus in need. Reach out and we'll guide you.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended so we can give your dog unhurried, individual attention. Use the booking form below or call us to reserve a slot.

How long does grooming take?

Typically 1.5–3 hours depending on the package and coat condition. We never rush — a calm experience matters more than a fast one.
